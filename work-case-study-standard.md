# Work case study design standard

Approved by Timothy on October 9, 2026. The [Operational AI local review](./operational-ai-drawer-review.html) is the reference for refining the remaining work pages, one at a time. Use its principles, not its exact arrangement, so each project can tell its own story.

## Story and hierarchy

- Build an editorial narrative with a clear beginning, decisions, evidence, and an ending that shows what happened.
- Keep copy concise and let genuine interface or product evidence do much of the explaining.
- Keep case heroes open, without a chapter navigation rail. Chapter numbers belong alongside the story sections (approved October 10, 2026).
- Use a left/right text and visual relationship where it helps reading. Allow a full-width image or an edge-to-edge product moment when the story calls for it.
- Make the page feel spacious. Let type, scale, alignment, and negative space establish hierarchy. Use faint lines chiefly to mark chapter boundaries; avoid repeated vertical dividers, rails, and visible boxes around every image.

## Visual evidence

- Show approved source work faithfully. Use complete screens when context matters, and exact components or crops when a specific interaction is the point. Sanitize publication assets and verify every claim and sample value.
- Compose related pieces with intentional scale, stagger, overlap, soft elevation, and open space. Keep slightly rounded corners and subtle `#f1f1f1` borders on graphics where a border helps them read. Do not mechanically repeat one card arrangement across chapters or pages.
- Preserve enough product context to show where the work lives. Avoid extra labels or captions that restate what the surrounding story already says.

## Interaction and motion

- Add a small number of meaningful interactive moments that demonstrate how the product works. The Operational AI search preview and sliding Assistant drawer are examples, not mandatory patterns.
- Stage text and visuals together as a reader reaches a chapter. Use movement, depth, and occasional scroll-linked scale to reveal the work; keep the final state clear and readable. Let featured quotes receive a distinct, restrained reveal when it serves the story.
- Make motion noticeable and smooth without competing with the interface. Keep ordinary scrolling native. Support reduced-motion preferences with a complete static presentation.
- Adapt every composition for narrow screens rather than simply shrinking desktop screenshots. Keep controls usable and source details accessible at full size when necessary.

## How to apply it

- Review each remaining work page with Timothy one at a time. Propose the story and visual treatments in a local preview, then refine them against the approved source material and the site's existing design language.
- Do not publish a page solely because this standard is approved. Confirm the specific page and its final assets before changing the public route.
